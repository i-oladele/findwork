# Project owner: required before public launch

Prepared November 13, 2026. These items cannot be completed by the developer using invented company information.

- Renew/activate Apple Developer membership. Confirm the enrolled legal entity, Apple Team ID, app ownership and developer access in App Store Connect.
- Confirm the Google Play account type, verification status and production access. A new personal account may require the 12-testers/14-day closed test before applying for production access.
- Provide legal entity name, RC number (if a registered company), registered/contact address, support email, privacy contact and payment partner legal name.
- Have counsel review `src/legal/documents.ts`: every bracketed placeholder, retention period, refund policy, fund custody statement and liability term needs a real decision. Do not remove the draft flag until approved.
- Confirm the licensed payment partner arrangement, live Paystack capabilities, refunds, settlements, KYC/AML obligations and applicable Nigerian privacy obligations with the relevant advisers/partner.
- Supply dashboard access through invitations, not shared passwords. Configure production credentials in the deployment environment rather than sending keys in chat.
- Create Firebase Android/iOS apps for `africa.findwork.app`; provide the client configuration files. Configure the APNs key in Firebase and the server FCM service account in Supabase secrets.
- Approve store name, descriptions, screenshots, distribution countries, pricing, content/age ratings, privacy answers and financial disclosures.
- Assign support, moderation, finance and verification staff, their response procedures and an SOS rota if SOS is offered.
- Approve production domain/hosting, backup/recovery budget and monitoring contacts.

Courses remain visible on both phones. Free enrollment and access to courses already owned remain available. New paid courses and plan purchases are available only on the website until store billing is implemented. No claim is made that this change alone guarantees store approval.

Target: prepare and test submissions as soon as the above permits. Public store availability by November 15 cannot be guaranteed because account verification, required testing and store review are external dependencies.
