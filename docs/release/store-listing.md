## Name

FindWork

## Apple subtitle / Google short description

Local services and shopping

## Description

FindWork helps you find local service providers and shop from sellers in Nigeria.

Browse providers, compare services, post a job and discuss the details before booking. Keep conversations, booking updates and order history together. Providers can manage their services and respond to requests; sellers can manage goods and orders.

Explore courses, enroll in free learning and continue courses you already own. New paid course and plan purchases are unavailable in the phone apps.

Report suspicious content and block abusive users. Manage your account and preferences from Settings.

Available payment, verification and delivery options depend on the services enabled for your account and location. Review prices, provider details and applicable terms before confirming a transaction.
